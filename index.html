How it works

Three steps to breed enlightenment

1

Open the scanner

Point your camera at the culprit, or upload a photo from your camera roll. Rear camera preferred, dramatic lighting optional.

2

On-device AI takes a look

A neural network runs entirely on your phone and guesses the breed about once a second. Nothing is sent anywhere.

3

Get the verdict

Top three guesses with confidence bars, plus a quip. The quip is arguably the best part.
